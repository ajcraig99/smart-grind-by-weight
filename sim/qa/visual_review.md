Screenshots were opened and looked at (PNG files in `sim/qa/out/`; the six committed ones in `sim/qa/screenshots/`). What each shows, as of the build under test:

- **Initial dashboard (1280x800, `s0_layout_full`)**: header with seed/target/beans/purge inputs and speed buttons, then the virtual screen (phone frame showing "DOUBLE 18.0g" and a red Play button) beside the 3D twin card, then mass balance, physical actions, fault injection, four trace plots (empty axes with a short flat line for the first 7 s), state diagram with IDLE highlighted, empty history table, plant parameter sliders, firmware log. Nothing clipped or overlapping; the left card keeps the same width when the state changes (an earlier build widened it by about 55 px while grinding and squeezed the plots; the suite checks this).
- **Normal 18.0 g, completed (`s1_normal_completed_*`, `screenshots/01`, `02`)**: the virtual screen shows the firmware's completion screen: "DOUBLE", "Target: 18.0g", an orange-red ring filled to the top with "18.0g", and a green round OK button with a white tick. Diagram: COMPLETED highlighted (teal) with green arrows along the path taken (IDLE, INITIALIZING, SETUP, TARING, TARE_CONFIRM, PRIME, PRIME_SETTLING, PURGE_CONFIRM, PREDICTIVE, PULSE loop, FINAL_SETTLING). Traces show the weight rising in a straight ramp to the dashed 18 g target line, a few short corrective pulses, and the error plot settling inside the +/-0.03 g band; the history row reads SUCCESS, green, final 17.98 g, true cup 17.970 g (shown in italics until the cup settles), 3 pulses, motor 10.73 s. The 3D view shows the cup with a pile of grounds, a few beans left in the hopper, and the relay LEDs.
- **Run-dry (`s2_rundry_*`, `screenshots/03`, `04`)**: the virtual screen shows "ERROR", "No beans?", a partly filled ring with "15.6g" and an amber round button with a white cross. The diagram highlights TIMEOUT in red (an earlier build of the page drew it in the same teal as every other active phase; the suite checks this), with the dashed 'any active phase' arrow in green. History shows "ERROR (TIMEOUT)" in italics (its last column 'Start s' is cut off at 1280 px, the table scrolls sideways), "No beans?", firmware final 15.61 g, true error -2.402 g in red. The hopper in 3D is empty.
- **Cup removed (`s3_cupremoved_*`)**: the virtual screen shows "ERROR", "Err: neg wt" and "-60.7g" with an empty grey ring and the same amber button; history shows the error text.
- **Manual taps (`s5_manual_*`)**: idle screen "DOUBLE 18.0g" with the red Play button; after a tap the purge prompt (white text "Previous grind time unknown. Remove the purge grinds if desired.", an "Always keep" checkbox, red Stop button on the left and green Continue button on the right); the grinding screen with a growing red ring, the weight and a red Stop button with a white square; after Stop the screen is back to the idle screen.
- **3D twin (`s6_3d_*`, `screenshots/05`, `06`)**: stylised grinder built from primitives (translucent hopper with bean models, burr housing, chute, body with a small screen texture, weighing platform with a transparent cup, blue relay box with two LEDs, floor grid). While grinding a column of light-tan particles falls from the chute into the cup and the weight on the texture counts up. The exploded view separates hopper, burrs, chute, motor, controller board (ESP32-S3 + display), weighing platform, HX711 board, load cell bar and relay + LEDs, each with a readable label. Orbit, Reset view and Hide 3D work.
- **1024x768 (`1024x768_s0_narrow_*`)**: single-column layout, no horizontal scroll, no overlapping cards; the header wraps onto three rows. The 3D canvas stays empty until it is scrolled into view (it does not render off-screen, by design), then renders normally.
- **Fault buttons (`s7_faults_*`)**: seven buttons plus the noise multiplier input; an active fault turns the button red with a pink label, but only when the pointer is not over it (see defects).
